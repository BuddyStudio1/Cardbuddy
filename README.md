# Cardbuddy
Storecards on your phone

Tired of a wallet overflowing with plastic loyalty cards? Meet CardBuddy, the clean, fast, and modern way to keep all your rewards cards right on your phone—and your wrist!

CardBuddy is designed to be your best friend at the checkout. No more fumbling through cluttered apps or physical wallets. Just scan, save, and go.

Key Features

•Instant Barcode Generation: Simply scan your physical card or type the number. CardBuddy generates high-quality barcodes (Code 128, QR Codes, ITF) that work with any scanner.

•Magic Auto-Branding: Type a store name (like Albert Heijn, IKEA, or Sephora), and CardBuddy automatically applies the brand’s colors and logo for you.

•Home Screen Shortcuts: Pin your most-used cards directly to your phone's home screen for one-tap access.

•Smart Categorization: Organize your cards by Supermarket, Fashion, Fuel, Electronics, and more.

•Notes & Perks: Store your member tier, points balance, or special perks in the notes section of each card.

•Secure Backup: Export your collection to a backup file and import it on any device. Your data stays with you.

•Privacy First: CardBuddy works entirely offline. Your card data and photos never leave your device.
